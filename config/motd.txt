<p>Welcome aboard, Crew!</p>
<p>You're joining <b>Nova Station</b>, a community-driven Space Station 13 project hosted by Circuit Verse Community. Our goal is to create an enjoyable, immersive, and evolving station experience for everyone.</p>
<p>Whether you're a seasoned spaceman or a newcomer stepping onto the station for the first time, we're glad to have you with us!</p>
<h2>Development Status &mdash; Work in Progress</h2>
<p>Nova Station is currently under active development. Our team is working toward building and improving the server, introducing planned modifications, and developing new features.</p>
<p>Please understand that development may progress slowly at times. We are a developing project, and improvements will take time. Some features may be incomplete, unavailable, or subject to change.</p>
<p><b>We appreciate your patience, understanding, and continued support as Nova Station grows.</b></p>
<h2>Codebase &amp; Development</h2>
<p>Nova Station is built upon the <b>Paradise Station codebase</b>, which serves as the foundation for our project.</p>
<p>Our development team intends to introduce custom modifications, improvements, and additional features to help establish Nova Station's own identity and gameplay experience.</p>
<p><b>Project repositories:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Original Paradise Codebase: <a href="https://github.com/ParadiseSS13/Paradise">Paradise Station GitHub</a></li>
<li>Nova Station Current Code: <a href="https://github.com/JohnnyM122/nova-station-circuit-verse">Circuit Verse &mdash; Nova Station GitHub</a></li>
</ul>
<p>Please remember that the server is actively evolving. Existing mechanics and features may be modified as development continues.</p>
<h2>Server Rules &amp; Expectations</h2>
<p>At this time, Nova Station does not have a formally established or finalized server rulebook.</p>
<p>Until official rules are introduced, all players are expected to exercise common sense, maturity, and respect toward one another.</p>
<p>Please keep the following expectations in mind:</p>
<ul>
<li>Treat fellow players and staff with respect.</li>
<li>Avoid harassment, intentional disruption, and unnecessary hostility.</li>
<li>Do not exploit bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics.</li>
<li>Use good judgment when interacting with other players.</li>
<li>Help maintain an enjoyable environment for everyone.</li>
<li>Follow reasonable instructions from authorized staff members.</li>
</ul>
<p>A lack of a finalized rulebook does not mean there are no expectations for player conduct.</p>
<h2>Staff Authority &amp; Enforcement</h2>
<p>Nova Station staff members are responsible for maintaining a welcoming, fair, and enjoyable environment.</p>
<p>Staff reserve the right to investigate incidents and issue disciplinary action when they determine it is necessary to protect the server and its community.</p>
<p>Depending on the circumstances, disciplinary action may include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Verbal or written warnings.</li>
<li>Removal from the server.</li>
<li>Temporary suspensions or bans.</li>
<li>Permanent bans.</li>
</ul>
<p>Staff decisions will be based on the circumstances of each situation and the overall interests of the community.</p>
<p><b>Please respect staff decisions and cooperate with their instructions.</b></p>
<h2>Help Us Build Nova Station</h2>

<p>We're building this station together, one step at a time.</p>
