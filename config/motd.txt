<h2>A Work in Progress &mdash; Please Be Patient</h2>
<p>Nova Station is currently under active development. We're working behind the scenes to improve the server, implement new features, resolve bugs, and build an enjoyable experience for everyone.</p>
<p>Please understand that development is currently progressing at a slower pace. This is an ongoing project, and updates may not always arrive as quickly as we'd like. We appreciate your patience, understanding, and continued support while we work toward our vision.</p>
<h2>Codebase &amp; Development</h2>
<p>Nova Station is built upon the <b>Paradise Station codebase</b>, which serves as the foundation for our server.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Original Codebase:</b> <a href="https://github.com/ParadiseSS13/Paradise">Paradise Station GitHub</a></li>
<li><b>Nova Station Current Code:</b> <a href="https://github.com/JohnnyM122/nova-station-circuit-verse">Circuit Verse &mdash; Nova Station GitHub</a></li>
</ul>
<p>While we currently utilize Paradise Station's existing code, we have plans to introduce our own modifications, improvements, and custom features as development progresses.</p>
<p>Our goal is to gradually establish a distinct identity and experience for Nova Station while building upon the existing foundation.</p>
<p>Please remember that some features may be unfinished, modified, or subject to change as development continues.</p>
<h2>Server Rules &amp; Expectations</h2>
<p>At this time, Nova Station does not have a formally established set of server rules. However, that does not mean anything goes.</p>
<p>Until official rules are published, we ask all players to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Use common sense and good judgment.</li>
<li>Treat fellow players with respect.</li>
<li>Be considerate of others and avoid intentionally ruining their experience.</li>
<li>Do not exploit bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics.</li>
<li>Avoid harassment, excessive toxicity, and disruptive behavior.</li>
<li>Respect the decisions and directions of server staff.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Staff Authority &amp; Enforcement</h2>
<p>Although formal rules have not yet been established, server staff retain full authority to address disruptive behavior and issue punishments as they deem necessary.</p>
<p>Depending on the circumstances, staff may issue verbal warnings, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans.</p>
<p>Staff decisions are intended to protect the community and maintain a welcoming environment. Please cooperate with staff when concerns arise.</p>
<h2>Help Us Build Nova Station</h2>
<p>We're building more than just a Space Station 13 server. We're building a community.</p>
<p>Feedback, suggestions, bug reports, and constructive criticism are always welcome. If you encounter an issue or have an idea that could improve the experience, please reach out to a member of the staff team or visit our Discord.</p>
<p>We are also interested in individuals who would like to contribute to the community and help shape its future.</p>
<h2>Stay Connected</h2>
