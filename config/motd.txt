<h2>Codebase &amp; Development</h2>
<p>Nova Station is currently built upon the <b>Paradise Station</b> codebase, which serves as the foundation for our project.</p>
<p>Our development goals include maintaining a stable server environment, improving gameplay, introducing custom features, and gradually building a unique Nova Station experience.</p>
<p><b>Project Repository:</b><br>
<a href="https://github.com/JohnnyM122/nova-station-circuit-verse">https://github.com/JohnnyM122/nova-station-circuit-verse</a></p>
<p><b>Original Codebase:</b><br>
<a href="https://github.com/ParadiseSS13/Paradise">https://github.com/ParadiseSS13/Paradise</a></p>
<p>We appreciate the developers and contributors whose work makes projects like Nova Station possible.</p>
