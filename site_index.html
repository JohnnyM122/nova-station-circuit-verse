Mission, Vision & Governance

Circuit Verse was created by players, for players. Whether you're here to roleplay in space, build together in Minecraft, or chill to good music, you're always welcome.

🎯

Mission Statement

To bring people together through the games and music we love—creating a welcoming, drama-free home where everyone can relax, make real friends, and share unforgettable adventures.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the community you always look forward to hanging out in—an open, close-knit corner of the internet where good vibes, shared creativity, and genuine friendships thrive every single day.

⚙️

Independent bare-metal and cloud nodes on Hetzner Cloud and Namecheap with Cloudflare Universal SSL, 99.9% uptime targets, automated cross-service role synchronization, and zero third-party telemetry.

Circuit Verse Organization Chart

Community leadership oversees the organization, while each individual service maintains its dedicated management structure and operational responsibilities.

🗳️

Server Host Democratic Governance & Operational Mandate

A Server Host (for Nova Station 13, Minecraft, or Project Zomboid) can be elected democratically by the community (primarily active server players) or appointed by Community Executives / Management if an election vote is not feasible. Holding the title of Server Host does not necessarily mean that the individual is a Community Executive or physical hosting infrastructure provider; they are trusted, community-elected operational leaders dedicated to representing their server's players and day-to-day gameplay integrity.

🏛️

Executive Staff & Management Staff Governance Structure

• Executive Staff (Top Governance Tier): Founder(s) (Organization ownership & final authority), Chief Community Officer (CCO) (Organization-wide executive oversight), and Community Director (Community operations and staff leadership).
• Management Staff (Operational Management Tier): Executive Director (Department coordination and execution), Department Directors (GC, MC, NS13, CV Radio, PZ) (Individual service and department leadership), and Community Management.
• Support & Community Leadership: Community Support Staff (Frontline assistance answering community questions and replying to support tickets) and Community Ambassadors (Recognized non-staff community leaders who welcome new arrivals and host events).
⚠️ Note on Executive Director: Despite the name containing "Executive", the Executive Director is part of Management Staff and is NOT part of Executive Staff.
